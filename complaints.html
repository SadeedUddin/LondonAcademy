Complaints policy

How to raise a concern with us, and what happens next.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Our commitment

We want every learner and enquirer to have a good experience. If you are unhappy with any part of our service, please tell us so we can put it right and learn from it.

Stage 1 — informal resolution

Many concerns can be resolved quickly. Please speak to your tutor or contact our team at info@londonukacademy.uk or +44 7435 476020.

Stage 2 — formal complaint

If the issue is not resolved, send a written complaint to info@londonukacademy.uk with the subject “Formal complaint”. Please include:

  • your name, contact details and course (if you are a learner);
  • what happened, when, and who was involved;
  • any evidence that supports your complaint;
  • what outcome you would like.

We will acknowledge your complaint, investigate it fairly, and send you a written response explaining our findings and any action we will take.

Stage 3 — review

If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you can ask for your complaint to be reviewed by a senior manager who was not involved in the original decision. Please request a review in writing and explain why you disagree with the outcome.

Assessment decisions and appeals

If your complaint is about an assessment decision, please ask us for our appeals procedure. Appeals are handled separately from general complaints.

Escalating further

If your complaint relates to a qualification and you have completed our process, you can raise it with the awarding organisation.

Fair treatment

Complaints are handled confidentially. Making a complaint will not affect how you are treated or assessed.
