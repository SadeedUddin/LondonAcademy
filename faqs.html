Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our qualifications, online study and enrolment.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

About our qualifications

Who awards your qualifications?
Our qualifications are awarded by recognised UK awarding bodies.
Which qualification is right for me?
It depends on your current role and experience. An enrolment advisor will recommend a suitable level before you enrol — book a free consultation.
Will I receive a certificate?
Yes. When you have met all assessment requirements, your certificate is issued by the awarding organisation.

Studying with us

Can I study fully online?
Yes. Learning materials, tutor support and assessment submissions are all online, so you can study from anywhere in the world.
How long does a qualification take?
Typical durations are 1–3 months for the Health & Safety Award, 3–6 months for the AET, 6–12 months for the Level 3 Diploma and CET, 6–9 months for the CPT and 12–18 months for the Level 5 Diploma. See our courses.
Will I have a tutor?
Yes. Every learner is supported by a dedicated tutor who reviews your work and gives feedback.
How am I assessed?
Most qualifications are assessed through written assignments and portfolio evidence, reviewed by your tutor and quality assured before certification.

Enrolment and fees

How do I enrol?
Send us an enquiry. An advisor will confirm suitability, explain fees and send you your enrolment details.
How much does a course cost?
Fees depend on the qualification. Contact us for current fees and payment options.
Can I cancel after enrolling?
Yes. If you enrol online as a consumer you normally have a 14-day cancellation period. See our terms & conditions.
Who do I contact if something goes wrong?
Talk to your tutor or email info@londonukacademy.uk. You can also follow our complaints policy.
