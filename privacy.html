Legal

Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Who we are

London Academy of Leadership and Management Limited (“we”, “us”) is registered in England & Wales and provides online qualifications. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about your data at info@londonukacademy.uk or +44 7435 476020.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, phone number, the course you are interested in and any message you send us.
  • Learner accounts: your name, email address, sign-in details, course enrolments, progress, assessment submissions and results.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends when you visit the site, such as your IP address and device type.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and advise you on suitable qualifications.
  • To enrol you, deliver your course, provide tutor support and assess your work.
  • To register you with the awarding organisation and arrange certification.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and quality-assurance obligations.
  • To keep our website and learner portal secure.

Our lawful bases

We process your data where it is necessary to take steps before entering into, or to perform, a contract with you; to comply with legal obligations; for our legitimate interests in running and improving our services; and, where required, with your consent (which you can withdraw at any time).

Who we share it with

  • Awarding organisations to register learners and issue certificates.
  • Service providers who host our website, deliver email and forms, or support our learner portal, under contract and only on our instructions.
  • Regulators and authorities where the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data.

International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside the UK. Where this happens we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, as required by UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including any period required by awarding organisations, regulators or law. Enquiry data that does not lead to enrolment is deleted when it is no longer needed.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our learner portal stores a small amount of information in your browser to keep you signed in. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or complete it;
  • ask us to delete it or restrict how we use it;
  • object to our processing, and to data portability where it applies;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email info@londonukacademy.uk. We will respond within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
