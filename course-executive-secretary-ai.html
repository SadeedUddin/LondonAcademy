Executive Secretary / Administrative Professional in the AI Age

Executive Secretary / Administrative Professional in the AI Age is designed to equip executive secretaries, administrative professionals, and office managers with modern skills for working effectively in AI-enabled workplaces. The program explores Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, digital productivity tools, automated workflows, communication, information management, meeting management, and executive support.

Enquire about this course → Back to all courses

Course Objectives

By the end of the program, participants will be able to:

  • ✓Understand the impact of AI on modern administrative and executive support roles.
  • ✓Use Generative AI to improve daily administrative tasks.
  • ✓Create professional emails, reports, presentations, and business documents using AI.
  • ✓Apply AI for research, summarization, information extraction, and analysis.
  • ✓Improve calendar, meeting, and task management.
  • ✓Automate repetitive administrative workflows.
  • ✓Organize and manage digital information more effectively.
  • ✓Use AI to support communication and executive decision-making.
  • ✓Apply professional standards for confidentiality, data protection, and responsible AI use.
  • ✓Improve time management, productivity, and workplace efficiency.
  • ✓Develop an AI-enabled administrative workflow.
