Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence provides professionals and business leaders with the knowledge and practical skills required to transform data into meaningful insights and informed business decisions. The program covers the data analytics lifecycle, data preparation, visualization, business intelligence tools, reporting, performance measurement, and data-driven decision-making.

Enquire about this course → Back to all courses

Course Objectives

By the end of the program, participants will be able to:

  • ✓Understand the fundamentals of Data Analytics and Business Intelligence.
  • ✓Identify different types and sources of business data.
  • ✓Collect, organize, and prepare data for analysis.
  • ✓Analyze data to identify trends, patterns, and business insights.
  • ✓Use analytical techniques to support business decisions.
  • ✓Create meaningful reports and interactive dashboards.
  • ✓Develop and monitor Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
  • ✓Interpret data visualizations and communicate insights effectively.
  • ✓Identify opportunities for improving business performance through data.
  • ✓Apply data-driven approaches to strategic and operational decision-making.
