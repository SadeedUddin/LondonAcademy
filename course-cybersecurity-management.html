Cybersecurity Management & Governance

Cybersecurity Management & Governance provides professionals and business leaders with a practical understanding of how organizations can protect information, systems, digital assets, and business operations from cybersecurity threats. The program focuses on cybersecurity governance, risk management, policies, security controls, incident response, compliance, and leadership.

Enquire about this course → Back to all courses

Course Objectives

By the end of the program, participants will be able to:

  • ✓Understand the fundamentals of cybersecurity management and governance.
  • ✓Identify common cybersecurity threats, vulnerabilities, and attack methods.
  • ✓Assess and manage cybersecurity risks.
  • ✓Develop cybersecurity policies, procedures, and controls.
  • ✓Establish effective cybersecurity governance structures.
  • ✓Align cybersecurity strategy with business objectives.
  • ✓Understand security frameworks, standards, and compliance requirements.
  • ✓Develop incident response and business continuity strategies.
  • ✓Improve organizational security awareness and cyber resilience.
  • ✓Monitor and evaluate cybersecurity performance and controls.
  • ✓Support effective cybersecurity decision-making at management level.
