Leadership & People

Emotional Intelligence & Advanced Leadership Communication

This program develops the emotional intelligence, communication, and leadership capabilities required to lead people effectively in complex and changing organizational environments. Participants will learn how to understand and manage their own emotions, recognize the emotions and perspectives of others, build stronger professional relationships, communicate with clarity and influence, manage difficult conversations, resolve conflicts, and motivate teams.

Enquire about this course → Back to all courses

Course Objectives

By the end of the program, participants will be able to:

  • ✓Understand the principles and dimensions of Emotional Intelligence (EI).
  • ✓Develop greater self-awareness and emotional self-management.
  • ✓Recognize emotional triggers and manage reactions effectively.
  • ✓Improve empathy and understanding of different perspectives.
  • ✓Develop advanced verbal and non-verbal communication skills.
  • ✓Communicate with clarity, confidence, and executive presence.
  • ✓Build trust and stronger professional relationships.
  • ✓Adapt communication styles to different personalities and situations.
  • ✓Develop active listening and questioning skills.
  • ✓Manage difficult conversations and challenging workplace interactions.
  • ✓Apply emotional intelligence to conflict management and resolution.
  • ✓Strengthen negotiation, persuasion, and influencing skills.
  • ✓Motivate and engage individuals and teams through effective communication.
  • ✓Develop leadership communication strategies for organizational change and transformation.
